Day 1 — The Steward’s Covenant

Purpose: replace chaos with identity. Today you will define your relationship with money—so money stops defining you.

Lesson

Most people don’t have a money problem. They have an identity problem. If money is your identity, you will chase it, fear it, waste it, or worship it. In AGEx, money is reclassified:

Money is a tool for stewardship — to create order, protect progress, and build legacy.

Three Core Laws (Day 1)

  • Law 1: Money follows structure. Chaos consumes money.
  • Law 2: Discipline beats motivation.
  • Law 3: Proof beats claims. What you track, you control.

Reflection (Answer honestly)

  • When money enters your hand, what usually happens?
  • What is your biggest leak (food, data, impulse, family pressure, loans)?
  • What would change if you had a simple system?

Stewardship Action (Homework)

Your first proof is not a test. It is a covenant. Complete this action today and submit proof.

Write Your “5 Money Rules”

Create five rules you will obey for the next 21 days. Keep them simple and enforceable.

Required
5 rules written clearly
Proof
Screenshot or typed submission

Example rules:
• I will track every expense daily.
• I will not borrow money for vanity.
• I will save first before spending.
• I will pause 24 hours before impulse purchases.
• I will not spend without a weekly plan.
