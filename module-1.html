Module 1 — Identity & Stewardship

Money is not your master and not your enemy. It is a tool. This module rebuilds your foundation: identity, responsibility, and discipline.

Objective

Replace chaos with a clear stewardship identity and simple rules you can actually live by.

  • Understand money as responsibility, not pressure.
  • Identify your “leaks” — where money silently goes.
  • Create a personal Money Rulebook (5 rules).
  • Start a small discipline action today (not tomorrow).

Lesson Summary

Stewardship means: “I manage what I have with order, truth, and restraint.” The beginning of wealth is not money — it is discipline.

This Academy does not promise profit. It builds competence. Verified competence unlocks deeper guidance.
